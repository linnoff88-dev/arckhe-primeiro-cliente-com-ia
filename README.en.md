## What it is

A free kit for people learning artificial intelligence who want to try offering their first service. Choose a type of client, demonstrate a small improvement, talk with the person in charge, and measure the results before proposing the next project. The templates help you get started with a clear scope. You need an AI tool you know how to use and a willingness to talk with a business; you don’t need to install this kit.

## How to use

Open the guide and follow the four steps. Download the ZIP, fill out the worksheets, and open the CSV spreadsheets in your editor. Workshop examples are hypothetical. Templates and spreadsheets are in Portuguese.

## Scope

Educational material. It is not prospecting software, does not send messages, and does not guarantee clients or income. Origin and limitations in [FONTE.md](FONTE.md). Version1.0.0.
